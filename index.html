Support

Zombie Jackpot is free to play, with ads between runs, optional rewarded videos and optional in-app purchases: coin packs, premium looks, the Starter Pack, Remove Ads and VIP Club, a monthly subscription that renews automatically until you cancel it. Coins buy only cosmetic items, nothing sold changes how the climb plays, and every product has fixed contents: nothing random is sold.
