Privacy Policy

Zombie Jackpot (Android) · Effective date: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Zombie Jackpot (the “app”, package com.zombiejackpot.game) uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is developed and published by Burak Başoğul (“we”, “us”). Questions: bb6516801@gmail.com.

At a glance

  • No account, no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We have no servers. Your progress, coins, items, settings and play statistics are stored only on your device.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which uses your device’s advertising ID and similar data. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use any analytics or tracking tool of our own.

1. Data stored on your device

To save your game, the app keeps the following on your phone, in the app’s private storage (the app’s web storage and Android’s app preferences):

  • progress: best score and floor, coin balance, unlocked and equipped outfits, achievements, daily and episode progress;
  • settings: language, control mode, announcer voice and vibration on/off;
  • local play statistics used to balance the game (for example number of runs, floors reached, run length and what ended a run);
  • bookkeeping for ads, reviews and sharing (for example when the last ad was shown, when a review was last requested, the day of the last share bonus);
  • which in-app purchases you own, so that they keep working.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else by the app. If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android’s automatic backup may include the app’s data in your device backup in your Google account, so that your progress can be restored after a reinstall or on a new phone. That backup is stored and managed by Google under your Google account settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is funded by ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited): optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for an in-game reward (for example a second chance or double coins), and occasional full-screen ads between games.

When ads are requested and shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (such as the country or city) can be estimated;
  • interactions such as app launches, ad views, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information about the app and the ads SDK;
  • basic device information such as the device model, operating system version and language.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, to personalize ads (only where allowed, see below), for analytics and to prevent fraud and abuse. On supported Android versions the SDK may also use Android’s ads privacy features (Privacy Sandbox, such as Topics and attribution reporting). All of this data is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses data for advertising

The app is configured so that ads are suitable for a general audience (maximum ad content rating “PG”).

3. Consent and your ad choices

  • Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app shows Google’s consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. No ad is requested until the consent step is complete.
  • If you do not consent to personalized ads, the ads you see are non-personalized. Non-personalized ads are based on context, such as the app and your approximate location, and Google may still use limited data for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.
  • You can change or withdraw your choice at any time: in the game, open Settings → PRIVACY OPTIONS (shown where the consent form applies). If you withdraw consent, the app stops requesting ads that rely on it.
  • In your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some phones) you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads for all apps.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

The app may offer optional in-app purchases (for example removing the between-game ads, outfits or coin packs). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; we never receive your card or other payment details. Google Play tells the app what you bought (product, purchase status and a purchase token) so that the app can deliver and restore your purchase; this check happens on your device and the result is stored on your device only. In Google Play Console Google also gives us limited order information (for example order number, product, date, price and country) for accounting, refunds and support. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

5. Ratings and reviews

Now and then, after a good moment in the game, the app may ask Google Play to show its rating window (Google Play In-App Review). This window is run entirely by Google Play. The app does not learn whether you rated the game or what you wrote. Reviews you post are public on Google Play under your Google account, under Google’s terms.

6. Sharing

  • The Share button creates a picture of your result on your device (score, floor, outfit and a link to the game) and opens Android’s share window. You choose whether to share it and with which app or person. We do not receive anything you share unless you send it to us.
  • In Settings → SHARE STATS you can copy or share your play statistics (game statistics plus basic technical details: app version, platform, screen size, language and a shortened browser engine version). Nothing is sent unless you choose to share it. If you send it to us, we use it only to improve the game’s balance and delete it when it is no longer needed.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

7. Device permissions

The app uses internet access (to load ads and to talk to Google Play) and vibration (for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings). The ads SDK adds the permissions it needs to read the advertising ID and use Android’s ads privacy features. Google Play adds the billing permission for purchases. The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.

8. Children

Zombie Jackpot is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not ask for any personal information from anyone. Ad requests are not tagged as directed to children, and ad content is limited to a general audience (“PG”). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

9. Keeping and deleting data

  • We do not keep any of your data on servers of our own.
  • The game data on your device stays until you delete it: Android Settings → Apps → Zombie Jackpot → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. A copy in your Google backup is managed in your Google account and device backup settings.
  • Data collected by Google for ads is kept by Google according to its policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings.
  • Emails you send us are deleted when they are no longer needed to help you, or earlier on request.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) or US state privacy laws) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store personal data about you, most of these requests concern data processed by Google; you can exercise them with Google directly (see the links above) and through the ad choices described in section 3. You are always welcome to contact us at bb6516801@gmail.com, and you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Security and international transfers

Game data on your phone is protected by Android’s app sandbox. Data sent by the Google SDKs is encrypted in transit (TLS). Google may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under appropriate safeguards described in its privacy policy.

12. Changes to this policy and contact

If the app’s use of data changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. Contact: Burak Başoğul · bb6516801@gmail.com
