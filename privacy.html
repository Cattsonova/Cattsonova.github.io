Privacy Policy

Zombie Jackpot (iPhone and Android) · Effective date: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Zombie Jackpot (the “app”; on the App Store “Zombie Jackpot: Tower Jump”; app ID com.zombiejackpot.game on iPhone and Android) uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is developed and published by Burak Başoğul (“we”, “us”). Questions: bb6516801@gmail.com.

At a glance

  • No account needed to play. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number. Only the optional online play uses a game account: a guest account or your Game Center / Google Play Games sign-in, with a player name you choose (section 16).
  • Your game is saved on your device. Progress, coins, items and settings stay on your phone. Our game server is used only by online play (section 16) and, if you allow it, by the cloud backup (section 17).
  • Gameplay analytics and crash reports (Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics) tell us how the game is played and when it breaks: game events with a random app ID, never your name, email or advertising ID. Where the law requires it, only with your consent; you can turn it off in Settings → GAMEPLAY ANALYTICS.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which uses your device’s advertising ID and similar data. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first (and on iPhone, Apple’s tracking prompt decides whether the advertising ID may be used), and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Optional in-app purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) on iPhone, where RevenueCat checks the purchase status for us, and by Google Play on Android. We never see your payment details.
  • Notifications are optional. Reminders are planned on your phone; announcements use Firebase Cloud Messaging. You can turn them off in Settings → NOTIFICATIONS.
  • We do not sell your data, and analytics are never used for advertising or to track you across other apps.

1. Data stored on your device

To save your game, the app keeps the following on your phone, in the app’s private storage (the app’s preferences and files):

  • progress: best score and floor, coin balance, unlocked and equipped outfits and items, achievements, daily and episode progress;
  • settings: language, music and sound effects, control mode and left-handed controls, announcer voice, vibration and notifications on/off;
  • local play statistics used to balance the game (for example number of runs, floors reached, run length and what ended a run);
  • bookkeeping for ads, reviews, sharing and notifications (for example when the last ad was shown, when a review was last requested, when the last weekly share bonus was paid, which reminders are planned);
  • which in-app purchases you own and whether a VIP Club subscription is active, so that they keep working;
  • a performance log (how smoothly the game ran: frame times, the game screen and floor at the time, and basic device details such as the phone model, Android/iOS version and screen size), kept only for the current and the previous session.

The app does not send this saved data to us or to anyone else. (Separately, gameplay analytics and crash reports are sent as described in section 2, and purchases are checked as described in section 5.) If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android’s automatic backup may include the app’s data in your device backup in your Google account, so that your progress can be restored after a reinstall or on a new phone. That backup is stored and managed by Google under your Google account settings. On iPhone, see section 15.

2. Gameplay analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

To see how the game is played and to fix problems, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, services of Google (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited) that act on our behalf as data processors. They send the following to Google’s servers, where only we can see it, as statistics in the Firebase console:

  • gameplay events: for example a run’s start and end, the floor and score reached, how long it lasted and what ended it, coins earned and spent in the game, episodes, the daily tower, achievements, which screens and in-game shop items were viewed, whether an ad offer was shown or accepted, store purchases (product and price, never payment details), settings changes, and coarse performance figures (a frame-rate range and the graphics quality level);
  • an app-instance ID: a random ID that the Firebase SDK creates for this installation. It is not your name or account. The app turns off the collection of the advertising ID (Android advertising ID; IDFA and IDFV on iPhone) and of the Android ID for analytics, and it does not set any user ID of its own;
  • device information: device model, operating system version, language, screen size, app version and the date of the first launch;
  • an approximate location (country, region or city) that Google derives from your IP address; the IP address itself is not stored by Google Analytics and is never shown to us;
  • crash and error reports (Crashlytics): technical details of a crash or a game error (where in the code it happened, the device’s state such as free memory and orientation, the game state such as the screen, stage and quality level, and the last game moments before it), with the Crashlytics installation ID. Error messages are cleaned of anything that looks like personal data before they are sent.

We use this only to improve the game: its difficulty and balance, the coin economy, performance on different phones, and to find and fix crashes. It is never used for advertising, never combined with other data to identify you, Google Signals is off, and we do not sell it. We never collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts or anything you type.

Consent. Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), analytics identifiers are only stored if you agree in Google’s consent form (the same form as for ads, see section 4); until then Google Analytics works in a restricted mode without identifiers (Google Consent Mode). You can change your choice in Settings → PRIVACY OPTIONS. Elsewhere, and for crash reports, we rely on our legitimate interest in a working, well-balanced game.

Turning it off. In the game, switch off Settings → GAMEPLAY ANALYTICS. Both Google Analytics and Crashlytics then stop collecting on your device, and crash reports not yet sent are deleted. You can switch it on again at any time.

Retention. Analytics event data is kept for 14 months and then deleted automatically (aggregated reports without any ID may remain). Crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for 90 days. Uninstalling the app resets the app-instance ID. See Privacy and security in Firebase.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free to play and is funded by ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited) and by optional purchases: optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for an in-game reward (for example a second chance or double coins), and occasional full-screen ads between games, never during a run. The Remove Ads purchase and a VIP Club subscription turn off the ads between games; the rewarded videos stay optional.

When ads are requested and shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • device identifiers: on Android the advertising ID and the app set ID; on iPhone the identifier for vendors (IDFV) and, only if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA, see section 15);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (such as the country or city) can be estimated;
  • interactions such as app launches, ad views, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information about the app and the ads SDK;
  • basic device information such as the device model, operating system version and language.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, to personalize ads (only where allowed, see below), for analytics and to prevent fraud and abuse. On supported Android versions the SDK may also use Android’s ads privacy features (Privacy Sandbox, such as Topics and attribution reporting). All of this data is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses data for advertising

The app is configured so that ads are suitable for a general audience (maximum ad content rating “PG”).

4. Consent and your ad choices

  • Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app shows Google’s consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. No ad is requested until the consent step is complete.
  • If you do not consent to personalized ads, the ads you see are non-personalized. Non-personalized ads are based on context, such as the app and your approximate location, and Google may still use limited data for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.
  • You can change or withdraw your choice at any time: in the game, open Settings → PRIVACY OPTIONS (shown where the consent form applies). If you withdraw consent, the app stops requesting ads that rely on it, and Google Analytics stops storing analytics identifiers (section 2).
  • On iPhone, Apple’s tracking prompt also applies (section 15).
  • In your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some phones) you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads for all apps.

5. In-app purchases (App Store, Google Play)

The app offers optional in-app purchases: coin packs, premium looks, the Starter Pack (an exclusive look and coins), Remove Ads (no more ads between games) and VIP Club, a monthly subscription that renews automatically until you cancel it. Every product has fixed contents; nothing random is sold. We never receive your card or other payment details.

  • On iPhone, payments are processed by Apple through the App Store and charged to your Apple Account. To find out what you bought and whether VIP Club is active, and to restore your purchases, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA), a service that acts on our behalf. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID that its SDK creates on your device (not your name, email address or Apple Account), the App Store transaction details (product, purchase, renewal and expiry dates, price and currency, storefront country and transaction IDs) and basic technical details (app version, iOS version and language); like any server the app connects to, it sees your IP address. We see these purchase records in RevenueCat’s dashboard and use them only to deliver and restore purchases, to handle refunds and support, and for accounting. Apple also gives us sales reports in App Store Connect. You can manage or cancel VIP Club in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. See the Apple Privacy Policy and the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • On Android, payments are processed entirely by Google Play. Google Play tells the app what you bought (product, purchase status and a purchase token) so that the app can deliver and restore your purchase; this check happens on your device and the result is stored on your device only. In Google Play Console Google also gives us limited order information (for example order number, product, date, price and country) for accounting, refunds and support. You can manage or cancel VIP Club in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

6. Notifications

The game can show two kinds of notifications. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → NOTIFICATIONS or in your phone’s settings.

  • Reminders are planned on your phone by the app itself (for example a few days after you last played, never at night). Nothing is sent to a server to plan them.
  • Announcements about the game are sent by us from the Firebase console through Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), on iPhone together with Apple Push Notification service. While notifications are on, the app receives a registration token for this installation and joins a topic that all players share; Google (and on iPhone, Apple) uses the token only to deliver the messages. We do not see who received a message, only totals such as how many were sent and opened. Turning notifications off deletes the token.
  • On iPhone and on Android 13 and newer, notifications start only after you agree in the game and allow them in the system dialog. On Android 12 and older, where the system does not ask, they are on from the first launch until you turn them off.

7. Shop settings and time check

  • Shop and coin settings (Android only). The Android version uses Firebase Remote Config (Google) to download a few settings that we set in the Firebase console: whether a launch offer is shown in the shop, which product it is and when it ends, and the coin rewards of runs. To fetch them, the app sends the Firebase installation ID and basic app and device details (such as the app version, language and country) to Google. These settings only switch or tune the offer and the coin rewards; they never download code or new content. The iPhone version does not use Remote Config.
  • Time check for the weekly share bonus. So that the weekly share bonus (section 9) cannot be claimed by changing the phone’s clock, the app reads the current date from Google’s servers: an empty HTTPS request to www.google.com/generate_204 (or www.gstatic.com/generate_204) whose answer carries the time. It sends no game data, ID or personal data; like any server, Google sees your IP address. The app makes this request a few times while it is open (for example when it starts and before a share bonus is paid) and keeps the result only in memory.

8. Ratings and reviews

Now and then, after a good moment in the game, the app may ask the store to show its rating window (Apple’s rating prompt on iPhone, Google Play In-App Review on Android). This window is run entirely by Apple or Google. The app does not learn whether you rated the game or what you wrote. Reviews you post are public in the store under your account, under the store’s terms.

9. Sharing

  • The Share buttons create a picture of your result or your best climb on your device (score, floor, outfit and a link to the game) and open your phone’s share window. You choose whether to share it and with which app or person. We do not receive anything you share unless you send it to us.
  • Once a week, a share that goes through earns a coin bonus. The app only learns whether the share window was completed, not where or with whom you shared.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

10. Device permissions

The app uses internet access (to load ads, to send gameplay analytics and crash reports, for purchases, notifications and the time check, and on Android for the shop settings) and vibration (for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings). On iPhone and on Android 13 and newer it asks for permission to show notifications; on Android it may also re-plan its reminders after the phone restarts. The ads SDK adds the permissions it needs to read the advertising ID and use Android’s ads privacy features. Google Play adds the billing permission for purchases. The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.

11. Children

Zombie Jackpot is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Apart from the player name you may choose for online play, the app does not ask anyone for personal information. Ad requests are not tagged as directed to children, and ad content is limited to a general audience (“PG”). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

12. Keeping and deleting data

  • Online play data and the cloud backup are kept and deleted as described in sections 16 and 17. Gameplay analytics are kept in Google Analytics for 14 months and crash reports in Crashlytics for 90 days, then deleted automatically (section 2).
  • The game data on your device stays until you delete it: on Android, Settings → Apps → Zombie Jackpot → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app; on iPhone, delete the app. A copy in your Google or iCloud backup is managed in your account and backup settings.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google and, for iPhone purchases, by RevenueCat for as long as they are needed to deliver and restore purchases and for accounting obligations.
  • A notification token is deleted when you turn notifications off or uninstall the app; Google also removes tokens that are no longer used.
  • Data collected by Google for ads is kept by Google according to its policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings; on iPhone, see section 15.
  • Emails you send us are deleted when they are no longer needed to help you, or earlier on request.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) or US state privacy laws) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Apart from online play and the cloud backup (sections 16 and 17, where you can delete your account and backup yourself), we do not keep personal data about you on servers of our own, so most of these requests concern data processed by Google, Apple or RevenueCat; you can exercise them with them directly (see the links above) and through the ad choices described in section 4. The analytics and crash data that reach us are linked only to a random app-instance ID, so we cannot tell which of it is yours: to stop it, switch off Settings → GAMEPLAY ANALYTICS; uninstalling the app resets that ID. You are always welcome to contact us at bb6516801@gmail.com, and you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

14. Security and international transfers

Game data on your phone is protected by the system’s app sandbox. Data sent by the SDKs in the app (ads, analytics, crash reports, notifications and purchases) is encrypted in transit (TLS). Google and RevenueCat may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under appropriate safeguards described in their privacy policies. Our online game server runs on Google Cloud in the United States (section 16); its connections are encrypted (TLS) and only we can access its data.

15. On iPhone (iOS)

The iPhone version works the same way, with these differences:

  • Tracking permission. Before ads start, iOS may show Apple’s “Allow Zombie Jackpot to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites?” prompt. Only if you choose Allow can Google use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) for ads; otherwise ads are still shown without it. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad measurement. The ads SDK may use Apple’s SKAdNetwork / ad attribution, which reports ad results to advertisers without identifying you.
  • Analytics. Gameplay analytics and crash reports work as in section 2. On iPhone, analytics never uses the advertising identifier (IDFA) or the identifier for vendors (IDFV), and the tracking prompt above is only about ads.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are made through Apple’s App Store and checked through RevenueCat (section 5); we never see your payment details. Subscriptions are managed in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.
  • No remote settings. The iPhone version does not use Firebase Remote Config: its shop and coin rewards are the same for every player and are only changed by an app update.
  • Notifications. iOS asks once whether the app may send notifications; you can change this in iOS Settings → Notifications → Zombie Jackpot or in the game’s Settings.
  • Storage and backup. Game data is kept in the app’s private storage on your iPhone and may be included in your iCloud or computer backup, depending on your iOS settings.
  • Ratings and sharing. The rating window is Apple’s (the app does not learn your rating), and sharing uses the iOS share sheet.
  • Deleting. Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone.

16. Online play and game accounts

Online play (live matches against friends, RANKED and practice) is optional. To play online you open a game account on our game server: a guest account, or your Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android) sign-in. Single-player progress and purchases are not part of it; a copy is kept only if you turn on the cloud backup (section 17).

  • What is stored: an account ID, the Game Center or Play Games player ID you signed in with, the player name you chose, your RANKED rating and match history, the replays of your matches (your inputs and their timing), the players you blocked, and the reports and appeals you send. To limit abuse the server keeps a coded network mark that changes every 14 days; it does not store your IP address.
  • Why: to run online matches and matchmaking, to show names and rankings to other players, and to prevent cheating and abuse (to provide the service you asked for, and our legitimate interest in fair play). Automated checks flag unusual matches; a sanction is decided by a person, and you can appeal it in the game.
  • Who sees it: other players see your player name, rank and match results. The data is stored on our server on Google Cloud in the United States, which processes it for us. It is not sold, not used for ads and not shared with anyone else.
  • Deleting: ONLINE → ACCOUNT → DELETE ACCOUNT or Settings → DELETE MY ACCOUNT AND SERVER DATA in the game, or by email (how to delete your online account). Your sessions end at once; your data is deleted within 24 hours and from backups within 7 days. Some records are kept without your identity (replaced by a code that cannot be turned back): sanctions, open review files until 180 days after they close, the audit log and rating ledger for 2 years, server logs for 14 days. An account nobody signs in to is deleted by itself: a guest account after 180 days, a Game Center or Play Games account after 24 months.

17. Cloud backup (optional)

The game asks you once: “Back up your progress on our server?” If you choose YES, BACK IT UP, or turn on Settings → CLOUD BACKUP, a copy of your game save is sent to our game server; without your permission nothing is sent.

  • What is sent: your coins, looks and items, best scores and floors, gameplay statistics, achievements, the app's own counters (for example ad and share counters, the time of the first launch) and the product names and times of your purchases; with the connection, the app version and the device model. Store payment tokens, your settings, your analytics consent and performance records are not sent.
  • Why: to keep a copy of your save, to be able to send you in-game gifts (such as coins or a look) and to help with support requests (such as a purchase that was not delivered). Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Account: the backup is linked to a game account; if you have not played online, a guest account with no name, visible to no one, is opened for it. The data is stored as in section 16, on our server on Google Cloud in the United States; it is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.
  • Turning it off and deleting: switch off Settings → CLOUD BACKUP and nothing more is sent. Settings → DELETE MY ACCOUNT AND SERVER DATA deletes your account and everything the server keeps, the backup included, as in section 16; the game on your phone stays. An unused guest account is deleted by itself, with its backup, after 180 days.
