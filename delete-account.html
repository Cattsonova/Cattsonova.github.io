Delete your online account

Zombie Jackpot (iPhone and Android) · online play

Online play (live matches against friends, RANKED) uses an account: your Game Center or Google Play Games sign-in, or a guest account, with the player name you chose. Single-player progress and purchases are not part of it: they stay on your phone and in your App Store or Google Play account, and deleting the online account does not touch them.

In the game (the quickest way)

  1. Open ONLINE → ACCOUNT.
  2. Tap DELETE ACCOUNT, read what is deleted and kept, tap CONTINUE.
  3. Confirm with YES, DELETE.

Or (1.1.0 and later) tap Settings → DELETE MY ACCOUNT AND SERVER DATA twice: if you never played online, the guest account opened for the cloud backup is deleted this way too.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game or the phone, send an email to bb6516801@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Zombie Jackpot account” and:

  • your account code (ONLINE → ACCOUNT shows it, for example K7Q2M-9XD4P), and
  • your player name as others see it (for example BORA#1234).

We check that the code and the name belong together, delete the account and answer you. Without the account code we cannot find a guest account; a Game Center or Play Games account can also be deleted by signing in to the game again on any phone and using DELETE ACCOUNT.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and player name, your sign-ins (the link to your Game Center or Play Games account), your RANKED rating and match history, your block list, and the replays of your matches.
  • Your cloud backup (if you turned it on) and gifts sent to you but not collected yet.
  • Reports other players made about you, unless a review is still open on them.

What is kept, without your identity

Some records must be kept to protect other players and to meet legal duties. In them your account is replaced by a code that cannot be turned back into it:

  • sanctions (and, while one is in force, a code of the Apple or Google account it applies to),
  • review files that are still open, until 180 days after they close,
  • the audit log and the rating ledger, for 2 years,
  • the server’s logs, for 14 days.

When

  • Your sessions end at once: your phones are signed out.
  • Your data is deleted within 24 hours, and from backups within 7 days.
  • A new account with the same Apple or Google account can be opened after 30 days.

An account nobody signs in to is deleted the same way by itself: a guest account after 180 days, a Game Center or Play Games account after 24 months.
